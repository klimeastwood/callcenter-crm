# CHANGELOG for CryptoCallCenter

## **v0.11.0 (19th of Oct 2026)**

* [fix] The operator now shares the company contact book without sharing the working log. Krayin's data scope was applied inside every contact controller (`preventUnauthorizedAccess($person->user_id)`), so an operator pinned to `individual` could not open a person or an organization created by a colleague, even though a call center picks every client from one common book. `Webkul\CryptoCallCenter\Bouncer` extends the core `Admin\Bouncer` and is registered under the string key `bouncer` (the key both `bouncer()` and the facade resolve), returning `null` - the core way of saying "no owner filter" - only on `admin.contacts.*` routes and only for accounts holding a declared package role; every other route falls back to the parent, so leads, activities and balance stay owner-scoped. The contact timeline (`admin.contacts.persons.activities.*`) is excluded on purpose: the card is shared, the colleague's calls and notes on it are not. The operator role also gained the missing `contacts.organizations.create`, `contacts.organizations.create.quick-create` and `contacts.organizations.edit` keys, without which the organization form of a colleague answered 401 despite the role description promising the shared book. `OperatorScopeTest` grew to 9 tests: both persons and organizations of a colleague appear in the grid, the foreign person card and organization form open, while the activity grid and the foreign person timeline stay 401 and the own timeline answers 200.

* [change] The moderator is pinned to `view_permission = global` instead of leaving the scope to the administrator. The role approves payouts and reviews deposits, so a per-account `individual` or `group` scope silently hid most of the desk; the `saving` enforcement now rewrites every moderator account to `global`, exactly as the operator is pinned to `individual`, and Settings -> Users cannot narrow it back. The global scope makes the core `getAuthorizedUserIds()` return `null`, so all leads, contact cards and activities of the desk are visible and `BalanceAdjustmentAccess` opens any lead through the existing `supervisor` flag. Settings, Users, Roles, Groups and Configuration still answer 401 and the Settings menu node is not rendered. `ModeratorRoleTest` now covers the global visibility of two operators' leads, contacts and activities, the scope rewrite, the denial of the administrative screens and the absence of Settings in the menu; the whole `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter` suite is 181 tests / 787 assertions green. No core files were changed.

* [fix] Closing a payout as «Выплачено» now actually takes the money out of the trader's balance. `approved → paid` used to change only the request status, so a completed payout left `trading_accounts.balance_usd` untouched and the client could withdraw the same money again. The debit lives on the trading side (`WithdrawalService::debit()`) and runs in the same transaction as the status change: the request row is already locked, the account row is locked as well so a trade, a deposit or a manual adjustment cannot overwrite the balance, and the amount is taken out once per transition - a replay finds the request already `paid` and answers `applied = false` before any money moves. The balance may not go below zero: a payout approved while the money was there can become unpayable after a losing trade, so the trading side refuses with 422 «Недостаточно средств: сумма вывода $… больше доступного баланса $…», the status rolls back with the debit, the payout stays `approved` and the operator retries after a top-up (the refusal reaches the screen word for word through the existing bridge path). The bridge response now carries `balance_before` / `balance_after` (`null` for an approval, a rejection and a replay); the CRM itself stores neither and keeps no money rules of its own. `WithdrawalReviewService` only forwards the call, and `DatabaseWithdrawalReviewGateway` now refuses `paid` with `withdrawals.errors.requires-bridge` (en + ru): an unreachable bridge with `CRM_TRADING_FALLBACK=true`, or `CRM_TRADING_DRIVER=database`, leaves the payout `approved` instead of closing it without a debit, while approvals and rejections still fall back to the shared table. `BridgeBalanceTest` grew to 18 tests / 115 assertions and covers the $4000-of-$4000 payout leaving `0`, the insufficient-funds refusal with a retry, the replay and the approval that moves nothing; `WithdrawalReviewTest` grew to 18 tests / 95 assertions: the refusal of a completion through the shared table, and the approved payout page that hides the completion form behind `withdrawals.errors.requires-bridge` when no bridge is configured. `TradingBridge::canClosePayout()` hides the form; the corrected `withdrawals.review.complete-hint` and `approve-hint` say that the trading side debits the balance when the payout is completed.

* [fix] Inbound mail reaches the CRM again. Krayin's `WebklexImapEmailProcessor` had three defects that together kept every message out of the inbox. It opened the IMAP connection in its constructor, and the container builds the processor just to register `inbound-emails:process`, so every artisan call that touched the container (`mail:test`, `about`, `migrate`) failed as soon as the mailbox was unreachable. It walked the folder tree down to its leaves and skipped every folder that had children, so a mailbox whose INBOX reports children never imported INBOX itself. And its folder map knew six English names only: `Sent Items`, `Junk`, `Отправленные`, `Спам`, `Корзина` were stored under an empty folder name, which no CRM screen renders. `InboundEmail/MailboxEmailProcessor` extends the core class and fixes all three without editing it: the constructor assigns the repositories directly (`parent::__construct` is not called), so the connection is opened on first use and `__destruct` disconnects only when it was opened; `processFolderTree()` visits every folder and recurses into `children`; `supportedFolder()` resolves English and Russian names through `FOLDER_ALIASES` (`inbox`, `sent`, `draft`, `trash`, `spam`, `important`, `starred`) and returns `null` for anything else, so an unmapped folder is skipped instead of being written invisibly (`All Mail`, `Archive`, `Архив` are ignored on purpose to avoid duplicates). `processMessage()` follows the core one but resolves the folder through the map, isolates a folder whose messages cannot be read (logged with `Log::warning`, the run continues) and gives a message without a `Message-ID` header a stable id (`uid.md5(folder path)@localhost`), so the duplicate check keeps working on every following run. A mailbox that cannot be reached is logged once and rethrown as `Inbound mail: <server reason>`, so the scheduler and the console print the server's answer instead of a stack trace through the IMAP library.
* [fix] `Support/MailboxConnection` reads the account the way the core processor does - Configuration → Email → IMAP first, the `imap.accounts.default` block (`IMAP_*` in `.env`) as the fallback - but parses `validate_cert` with `filter_var(..., FILTER_NULL_ON_FAILURE)`; the core cast turned a saved `0` or a `.env` `false` into `true`, so a self-signed certificate could not be accepted at all.
* [fix] The container binds `InboundEmailProcessor` to `MailboxEmailProcessor` from a `booted()` callback (`CryptoCallCenterServiceProvider::preferInboundMailbox()`): `bootstrap/providers.php` registers this package before `EmailServiceProvider`, whose `boot()` binds the same contract to the core processor, and a `booted()` callback runs after every provider has booted. The driver is read at resolution time, so `sendgrid` still resolves `SendgridEmailProcessor` and an unknown driver still throws.
* [feature] Added `php artisan mail:inbox` (`Console/Commands/CheckInbox`), the inbound counterpart of `mail:test`: it prints the resolved host / port / encryption / username / masked password and certificate flag, then the folder list with the CRM folder each one maps to, its message count and how many messages fall into the `--days=10` window, and finally the newest `--limit=5` messages per supported folder (UID, date, sender, subject). `--folders` stops after the listing, so a mailbox with a large INBOX is not fetched. Folder-level failures print a warning and the listing continues; a failed connection prints the server's reason plus a hint (mail.ru requires an application password and enabled IMAP access, port 993 speaks `ssl`, a self-signed certificate needs `IMAP_VALIDATE_CERT=false`). The password is never printed, only its length.
* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/InboundMailboxTest.php` (33 tests / 35 assertions): the folder mapping for English and Russian names (including `Входящие`, `Отправленные`, `Черновики`, `Корзина`, `Спам`, `Удалённые` and the `Sent Items` / `Junk E-mail` spellings), unmapped and empty names returning `null`, `All Mail` / `Archive` / `Архив` being ignored, the container resolving `MailboxEmailProcessor` for `webklex-imap` and `SendgridEmailProcessor` for `sendgrid`, the processor being built without opening a connection (the parent's `client` stays `null`, with the host pointed at an unreachable name), and the synthetic id being stable across runs and different per folder. No Krayin file is edited.
* [feature] The payout desk decides in the CRM instead of only reading. `POST admin/crypto-call-center/withdrawals/{withdrawal}/approve` and `.../reject` (`whereNumber`, `admin.crypto_call_center.withdrawals.approve` / `.reject`) close a staged payout: `WithdrawalController::approve()` takes an optional `note` (255 characters), `::reject()` a required `reason` (3-255), both declared in `Http/Requests/ApproveWithdrawalRequest` / `RejectWithdrawalRequest`, whose `authorize()` repeats the `withdrawals.card` check that the route map in `Config/acl.php` already applies - the desk that may read the number is the desk that closes the request, so a "may look but not decide" role never exists to assign. `Resources/views/withdrawals/card.blade.php` carries both forms while the request is pending (`withdrawals.review.*`), repeats `withdrawal_success` and the `withdrawal` error bag afterwards, and, once the request is decided, prints the operator, the moment and the note the client reads instead of the forms. The queue action is still only "open": the decision stays on the page that shows the requisites.
* [feature] `Services/Withdrawal/WithdrawalReviewService` is the only path that records a decision, and it moves no money: the back office still pays the payout, the CRM closes the request the client reads. A request that is not `pending` is refused before a socket is opened, and a whitespace-only note becomes `null` instead of an empty string. `Services/Withdrawal/WithdrawalReviewGateway` is bound to `HttpWithdrawalReviewGateway`, which posts `request_id` / `status` / `note` / `reviewer_id` to `api/withdrawals/review` with the shared Bearer token, passes a `422` refusal to the operator word for word (the trading side owns the transition rules, so repeating it locally would only duplicate the answer), and replaces only a missing configuration, a `ConnectionException` or a `5xx` by the direct write - and only when `crypto_call_center.trading.fallback` is enabled; otherwise the operator is told the request is still pending. `DatabaseWithdrawalReviewGateway` writes the shared row under `lockForUpdate()` and answers `false` when the row already carries that status, so a repeated delivery cannot move `reviewed_at` or overwrite the reason the client was given, while a different decision on a reviewed request is refused with its current status. `Exceptions/WithdrawalReviewException` and the two form requests keep every wording in the package translations (`withdrawals.review.*`, `withdrawals.errors.*`).
* [security] The CVV the client typed is printed for the operator who pays, and only there: `withdrawals/card.blade.php` shows `$withdrawal->card_cvv` next to the masked card and `deposits/show.blade.php` shows the code of the deposit (`deposits.show.cvv`), both read from the request row rather than from the saved card, so the queue, an export and the query log stay clean; the page itself is still behind `withdrawals.card` / `deposits.review`. A crypto payout carries no code, and a payout or deposit that reuses a saved card has none to show.
* [feature] A payout the back office has actually paid is now closed from the same page. `POST admin/crypto-call-center/withdrawals/{withdrawal}/complete` (`whereNumber`, `admin.crypto_call_center.withdrawals.complete`, `Http/Requests/CompleteWithdrawalRequest` repeating the `withdrawals.card` check that the route map applies) calls `WithdrawalReviewService::complete()`, which accepts only an `approved` request, answers an already `paid` one without a second call and refuses `pending` / `rejected` with `withdrawals.errors.not-approved`; the reviewer, the moment and the note the client was given are never rewritten, so closing a payout cannot change the decision. `withdrawals/card.blade.php` prints the complete form while the request is approved and the decided panel (operator, date, note) once it is paid, and the queue renders the new status through `datagrid/status.blade.php` with `label-active`. No money moves in the CRM - the payout itself is still a back-office action. `WithdrawalReviewTest` covers the transition, the idempotent replay, the refusal of an unapproved request and the permission gate.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/WithdrawalReviewTest.php` (12 tests / 53 assertions): the approval through the gateway and through the shared table, the rejection storing the reason, the decided page printing the operator and the reason with no form left, a second decision refused, an unreachable trading side with the fallback disabled and enabled, a repeated delivery answering without being applied again, the shape of the request the bridge receives, the refusal shown word for word, and the CVV printed next to the card of the payout; `DepositReviewTest.php` gained the matching CVV case (10 tests / 61 assertions). Both suites skip themselves when the trading tables are absent, and the bridge is always faked, so no live trading application is called. Run: 379 tests / 1119 assertions (whole CRM suite); `Pint` clean on 829 files.
* [fix] A valid email or a valid phone number typed into the lead form no longer answers with "Error validating email. Please try again." The form asked `admin.settings.attributes.check_unique_validation` whether the value was taken; that route declares no ACL entry of its own and therefore inherits the nearest mapped feature, `admin.settings.*`, so an operator who may create a lead but holds no Settings permission was answered with 401 - and the component printed its catch-all text under the email field and, through the same catch block, under the phone field as well. The question is now answered by `GET admin/leads/check-unique-contact` (`admin.leads.check_unique_contact`, `Http/Controllers/ContactUniquenessController`), named under the lead feature on purpose: the core Bouncer authorizes an unmapped route against its nearest mapped ancestor, so the check needs exactly the permission that already opens the form, while an account without any lead permission is still refused with 401. An own ACL key was rejected because it would have to be re-granted to every role provisioned before this release, and those roles would keep getting the 401 the route exists to remove. The lookup is also narrower than the core one: `entity_type` is allow-listed to `persons`, `attribute_code` to `emails,contact_numbers`, and the attribute is resolved by code *and* entity type, so the person phone number can no longer be compared against the warehouse attribute that carries the same code. `entity_id` stays optional - the edit case excludes the person being edited, a new person is compared with the whole contact book, and a missing id is turned into `0` because `isValueUnique()` excluding a null id would compare `entity_id != NULL` and call every value free. Two Blade copies (`Resources/views/overrides/components/attributes/edit/{email,phone}.blade.php`, already served through the `admin` view hint the package prepends) point the components at the new route and treat an unreachable check as "not verified" instead of rejecting the form: an auxiliary lookup must not block a correct form, the server-side `LeadForm` rules stay the authority, and a value typed twice in the same form is still caught locally. `LeadContactUniquenessTest` (4 tests): the operator is answered 200 instead of 401, a taken address is reported and excluded for the person that carries it, an account holding `contacts.persons` but no lead permission is still refused with 401, and the lead form stores a person with a fresh `lead-…@example.invalid` address and a `+37529…` number without a single validation error - after which the endpoint reports that stored address as taken, so the contact book and the check agree on the saved shape. Run: 390 tests / 1198 assertions green; `Pint` clean. No core files were changed.

## **v0.10.0 (18th of Oct 2026)**

The request is performed through `Services/Deposit/DepositBalanceGateway` (`http` over `POST /api/deposits/credit`, or `database` for an installation without a bridge); a 422 refusal of the trading side is passed through word for word and leaves the request pending, while a connection error or a 5xx falls back to the direct write only when `crypto_call_center.trading.fallback` is enabled. `Exceptions/DepositReviewException` and the two form requests (`note` optional, `reason` required and 3-255 characters) keep the wording in the package translations.

* [security] The trading clients are no longer offered as the Sales Owner of a lead. A self-registered client is a CRM user carrying the role the trading application creates for it, so `Support/TradingClients` reads that role (`crypto_call_center.trading.client_roles.names` / `.ids`, i.e. `CRM_TRADING_CLIENT_ROLES` / `CRM_TRADING_CLIENT_ROLE_IDS`, falling back to the names the trading side uses), `Models/SalesOwner` is the users table seen through a `staff-only` global scope and `Repositories/SalesOwnerUserRepository` is that model behind the `UserRepository` name the attribute lookup detects. `DataGrids/Overrides/LeadDataGrid` points the `sales_person` filter at it, and the provider rewrites the `users` lookup repository in `attribute_lookups`, so neither the owner filter of the lead list nor the selector of the lead form can hand a lead to a customer. ## **v0.9.0 (18th of Oct 2026)**

* [feature] The package declares a second role, "Call Center Moderator", next to the operator in `Config/roles.php`. A moderator is an operator that supervises, so its declaration starts from the operator one (`inherits => operator`) and only adds: permissions are unioned while every other key is overridden by the child, so a screen handed to the operator reaches the moderator on the next provisioning run instead of being copied by hand. The resolution lives in `Support/OperatorScope::definitions()`, which the provisioning command, the scope enforcement and the tests all read, so the two roles cannot drift apart.
* [feature] The operator role works the pipeline end to end: `leads.create` and its quick-create sibling, `activities.edit`, the contacts create/edit and person view keys, the `call_logs` screen and the `withdrawals` payout queue. Deleting a lead, editing somebody else's activity, Settings, Users, Roles, Configuration, Products and Quotes stay out of the role, and the unmasked payout card (`withdrawals.card`) stays with the moderator.
* [feature] A lead created by a role that asks for it belongs to the account that typed it in: the `assign_creator_as_lead_owner` flag of the declaration plus `Listeners/AssignLeadSalesOwner` on `lead.create.after`. Without it an operator - whose scope only shows the leads it owns - would create a lead and lose it the moment it was saved. The write is `saveQuietly()`, so creating a lead still leaves one timeline entry and not a second one about its owner, and an administrator, the web form or the API keep the owner they submitted.
* [security] A moderator may change the balance of the leads it can see, not only the ones it owns (`supervisor` in the declaration). `Support/BalanceAdjustmentAccess` gained that branch next to the ownership rule and `permission_type = all`, and it still requires the data scope to cover the lead (`bouncer()->getAuthorizedUserIds()` of the acting account), so a moderator kept on `individual` moves only its own money and being able to open a lead is still not a licence to move money.
* [feature] The moderator scope is deliberately not pinned: `view_permission` is `null` in its declaration, so the administrator chooses `group` or `global` per account in Settings → Users and the submitted value is kept - the `saving` listener only rewrites accounts holding a role that declares a scope. The operator stays on `individual`, enforced as before.
* [feature] `php artisan crypto-call-center:operator-role` provisions any declared role instead of only the operator: the role key is an argument (`{role=operator}`), `--all` provisions every declared role in config order (a parent before its child), `--name` / `--description` are refused together with `--all` because they describe a single role, and an unknown key fails with the list of declared roles. Every message names the role key it speaks about.
* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/ModeratorRoleTest.php` (12 tests / 77 assertions): the inherited permission set is a superset of the operator one, the moderator scope is left to the administrator (`OperatorScope::scope('moderator')` is `null`) while the operator is still pinned, the supervisor and creator-as-owner flags, the menu above the operator's, the reachable screens plus the revealed payout card, the lead listing under the global / group / individual scope, the balance rights of a supervising and of a plain moderator, `--all` provisioning both roles, and the refusal of `--all` combined with `--name` and of an undeclared key. `OperatorRoleTest` grew to 9 tests: the widened menu, the new screens, the payout card refused to the operator and a lead created by an operator belonging to it.

## **v0.8.0 (18th of Oct 2026)**

* [feature] Added `php artisan mail:test` (`Console/Commands/MailTest`, registered in the package provider) so a broken mail `.env` is found before a manager needs a quote or a password reset. The command prints the resolved transport - mailer, host, port, encryption, username, the masked password and the from/to addresses - and only then contacts the server, so a configuration mistake is named instead of surfacing as an SMTP stack trace: an empty `MAIL_HOST`, `MAIL_PORT`, `MAIL_USERNAME` or `MAIL_PASSWORD` fails the command with the name of the variable, and a missing or invalid `MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS` fails it as well. Transports that never authenticate (`log`, `array`, `sendmail`) are not asked for a mailbox. The password is never printed: the column shows its length, which makes the output safe to paste into a ticket. `--dry-run` validates the settings without sending, `--mailer=` verifies a candidate mailer before it becomes the default and `--timeout=15` bounds the wait, because `config/mail.php` leaves the timeout unset.

* [feature] Warnings for configurations that work until they do not: a cached `bootstrap/cache/config.php` makes an edited `.env` look ignored, an empty `MAIL_ENCRYPTION` on port 465 or 587 is almost certainly fatal, and a `MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS` that differs from `MAIL_USERNAME` is rewritten or rejected by a relay such as mail.ru.

* [fix] A failed send is explained rather than thrown: a 535 / authentication rejection names the mail.ru application password the mailbox requires, an unreachable host points at `MAIL_HOST` / `MAIL_PORT` / `MAIL_ENCRYPTION` and the provider's port blocking, and a certificate failure points at the `ssl` / `tls` choice. Any other error keeps the exception class and message and falls back to `storage/logs/laravel.log`.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/MailTestCommandTest.php` (8 tests / 34 assertions): the default desk address, the message reaching the mailer mock with the requested recipient, an invalid address refused before the transport is touched, the empty SMTP variables named, the password absent from the output, `--dry-run` sending nothing, the authentication hint and the encryption warning.
* [feature] The whole sign-in flow carries the CRYPTOISK logo instead of the Krayin wordmark. `Resources/views/overrides/sessions/{login,forgot-password,reset-password}.blade.php` mirror the shipped screens line for line except for the logo block, which becomes the shared `components/branding/session-logo` partial: it prints a logo uploaded in Configuration → General → Settings and otherwise the mark plus the wordmark (`h-12 w-12` / `text-2xl`), so a deliberate admin choice keeps winning exactly like in the header and the mobile drawer. The copies resolve through the `View::prependNamespace('admin', ...)` the header already uses, so no core file is touched; the "powered by" footer of those screens is still swapped by the provider's `View::composer('admin::sessions.*')`, not by the copies. The shipped `admin.sessions.forgor_password.*` event name is kept verbatim, typo included, so existing listeners still fire.

* [feature] The lockup is sized by its caller: `components/branding/logo.blade.php` accepts a `textClass` (default `text-lg`) next to the existing `markClass`, and the product name is now CRYPTOISK (`crypto_call_center::app.branding.name`, still one translation key per locale).

## **v0.7.0 (18th of Oct 2026)**

* [feature] The payout queue of the trading cabinet is readable from the CRM. The `withdrawal_requests` and `withdrawal_cards` tables belong to the trading application, so the CRM only reads them: `DataGrids/WithdrawalDataGrid` joins the client and their card, sorts the newest request first and filters by client, amount, method and status; `Http/Controllers/WithdrawalController::index` serves the grid over AJAX and `Resources/views/withdrawals/index.blade.php` otherwise; the route `GET admin/crypto-call-center/withdrawals` is `admin.crypto_call_center.withdrawals.index` and the `withdrawals` node is declared in `Config/menu.php` and `Config/acl.php`. `Repositories/WithdrawalRequestRepository::available()` checks both tables, so an installation that has not deployed them gets a sentence instead of a broken grid. Nothing is written back - approving and paying still happen in the trading back office.

* [security] The card number is opened on its own screen and only for an account that may reveal it. `GET admin/crypto-call-center/withdrawals/{withdrawal}/card` (`...withdrawals.card`, `whereNumber`, 404 for an unknown id) is gated by the second ACL key `withdrawals.card`, which is not implied by `withdrawals`: the queue prints `•••• •••• •••• 4242` with the brand (`Resources/views/withdrawals/datagrid/requisites.blade.php`) and never the number, so the list, an export and the query log stay clean. `Support/CardCipher` opens the `v1:base64(nonce):base64(tag):base64(cipher)` payload the trading side seals with the shared `WITHDRAWAL_CARD_KEY` (`crypto_call_center.trading.withdrawal.card_key`, AES-256-GCM, key derived with `hash('sha256', ...)`); a missing secret answers "not configured" and a foreign one "unreadable" because the GCM tag does not verify. `Models/WithdrawalCard` keeps `pan_encrypted` and `fingerprint` hidden, so a stray serialisation cannot leak them. A crypto wallet is public data and is printed in full, because the desk has to verify it.

* [feature] A payout request reaches the lead timeline as `withdrawal.requested`: `Models/TradingEvent::TYPE_WITHDRAWAL_REQUESTED`, the wordings in `Support/TradingEventPresenter` (`trading-events.activity.withdrawal` with the amount, `badge.withdrawal`) and `withdrawalRequisites()` - `**** 4242` with the brand for a card, `TQn9Y2…bLSE` for a wallet - so the event written by the trader's feed never carries a full number either. The badge is neutral (`label-inactive`): a payout is money leaving the account, and the green of a deposit stays for incoming money. `balance()` reads `balance_after ?? balance`, because the payout payload carries only `balance`.

* [fix] The requisites column no longer depends on a value another column has already rewritten: `$row->method` is replaced by its translated label while the method column renders, so the branch is now taken from the wallet and the card of the request and a crypto payout is not shown as empty. An unknown card brand falls back to `brands.unknown` and an unknown status prints its raw value instead of a translation key, on the queue and on the request page (`statusLabel()`).

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/WithdrawalFeedTest.php` (12 tests / 43 assertions): the masked number in the queue and the number never in the payload, the full wallet of a crypto payout, the queue refused without `withdrawals`, the reveal refused without `withdrawals.card` and served with it, the "Open request" action present only with the reveal permission, "not configured" for a missing key and "unreadable" for a foreign one, 404 for an unknown id, the contract binding and `findWithCard()`, the presenter wording for a card and a wallet, and the masked activity the listener writes into the lead. The payout tables are read from the shared database, so the cases skip themselves on an installation without them. `TradingEventFeedTest` now parks `kyc.requested`, because `withdrawal.requested` became a known type.

* [feature] A trader balance can be changed by hand from the lead card. The new "Trader balance" block (`Resources/views/leads/balance-adjustments.blade.php`) is inserted through the same `admin.leads.view.activities.before` hook the trading feed uses (`registerBalanceBlock()` in the provider), prints the current balance read by `Repositories/TradingAccountRepository::balanceFor()`, offers a signed amount and a reason, repeats the success line or the refusal of the trading side, and lists the five newest changes with their operator and date. It is rendered only when `Support/BalanceAdjustmentAccess::allows()` and `Support/TradingBridge::ready()` both say yes, so a card never offers a form that can only fail. No Admin view was edited.

* [feature] The CRM asks the trading side instead of writing a table it does not own: `Services/Balance/TradingBalanceGateway` declares the change, `HttpTradingBalanceGateway` sends it to `POST api/balance/adjust` with the shared Bearer token and the CRM row id as `adjustment_id`, passes a `422` refusal to the operator word for word, and replaces only a configuration error, a lost connection or a `5xx` by a direct write - and only when `crypto_call_center.trading.fallback` is enabled. `DatabaseTradingBalanceGateway` serves an installation without a bridge and repeats the rules of `TradingService::adjustBalance()` on purpose (no zero amount, no negative balance, the same ceiling, one event per CRM adjustment recognised by `payload.reference`), while `BalanceChange` reports the balances and whether the change was `applied` or already known. `Config/trading.php` is merged as `crypto_call_center.trading.*` from `CRM_TRADING_DRIVER`, `CRM_TRADING_URL`, `CRM_TRADING_TOKEN`, `CRM_TRADING_FALLBACK`, `CRM_TRADING_TIMEOUT` and `CRM_TRADING_MAX_AMOUNT`, and `registerBalanceGateway()` binds the contract to the transport the configuration names.

* [feature] Every change leaves an audit trail: the `crypto_balance_adjustments` migration, `Contracts/BalanceAdjustment`, `Models/BalanceAdjustment` (contract-backed proxy included) and `Repositories/BalanceAdjustmentRepository` (`latestForLead()`) store the lead, the trader account, the operator, the signed amount, the reason, the balances copied from the trading side and the id of the shared event the change was mirrored into. The row is written **before** the request, because its id is the idempotency key the trading side receives, and it is removed again when the request is refused, so the history never shows a change that did not happen. `lead_id` cascades with the lead, `user_id` and `admin_id` are nulled on delete: the history survives a deleted employee or trader account.

* [feature] The change reaches the timeline as one entry. `Services/BalanceAdjustmentService` writes a `system` activity ("Balance changed by +$1 000. Reason: ...") and, in the same transaction, reserves the mirrored `balance.adjustment` event in the ledger of the feed, so the minute scheduler does not write the same change a second time (a reservation the feed already owns wins and the link is left untouched). `Models/TradingEvent::TYPE_BALANCE_ADJUSTMENT` and `Support/TradingEventPresenter` word the event on its own - `adjustment-credit` / `adjustment-write-off` plus `signedMoney()` - so an adjustment that arrives through the feed alone is described like an operator would describe it.

* [security] A manual balance change is a separate permission, not a side effect of reading a lead: the new `crypto_call_center.balance` key in `Config/acl.php` maps to `POST admin/crypto-call-center/leads/{lead}/balance`, `Config/roles.php` grants it to the packaged operator role (align existing roles with `php artisan crypto-call-center:operator-role --sync`), and `Support/BalanceAdjustmentAccess` requires the key **and** either the sales owner of the lead or a role with `permission_type = all`. A group manager holding the key but not owning the lead gets `403`, does not see the block, and the key alone never becomes a licence to move somebody else's money; an owner whose role does not grant the key is stopped by the core bouncer with `401` before the request is validated. Nothing reaches the gateway on either path.

* [feature] The form works without JavaScript: `Http/Requests/BalanceAdjustmentRequest` validates a signed amount inside the ceiling of the trading side, refuses a zero amount in `after()`, translates the field names, and resolves the lead from the route model binding (a raw id is accepted as a fallback), while `Http/Controllers/BalanceAdjustmentController` redirects back to the card with either the applied line or the refusal of the trading side in `balance_adjustment`.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/BalanceAdjustmentTest.php` (11 cases / 60 assertions): a credit with the audit row, the redirect and the flash line, the gateway receiving the row id as its idempotency key, a negative write-off, a zero amount and an amount above the ceiling refused before the request leaves the CRM, a manager who holds the key but does not own the lead, an owner whose role lacks the key, a refusal of the trading side deleting the audit row again, a repeated delivery on the shared tables applying the change once, the mirrored event reserved in the feed ledger, and the block rendered for the owner and hidden from a foreign employee. The gateway is replaced through the container, so the CRM suite runs without the trading application; the two tests that need the shared tables skip themselves when the trading side is not deployed.

* [fix] The provisioning test creates its role under a unique name. A development database that already carries the packaged role from an earlier release made the assertion about the packaged description depend on that row, because the command leaves an existing role untouched; the test now always exercises a first-time provision.

## **v0.6.0 (17th of Oct 2026)**

* [feature] The trading application now reaches the lead timeline. `trading_events` is a log the trading service writes into the shared database; `Contracts/TradingEvent`, `Models/TradingEvent` (contract-backed proxy included) and `Repositories/TradingEventRepository` read it, while the new `trading_event_activities` migration records which row was already turned into an activity. The command `php artisan crypto-call-center:sync-trading-events [--limit=200]` reads the unprocessed rows, raises `Events/TradingEventReceived` for each of them and `Listeners/TradingActivityListener` writes a `system` activity into the card of the operator that owns the event, so the entry cannot be edited or deleted from the timeline.

* [feature] A deposit and an opened or closed position are worded by `Support/TradingEventPresenter`, which the timeline and the lead-card block share: the amount, the pair and the side are formatted in one place (`$1 000`, `0.5 BTCUSDT`, `$60 000`) and the message is stored in the panel language taken from `general.general.locale_settings.locale`, because the scheduler has no `admin_locale` middleware to set a request locale. `--limit` caps how many rows one run reads, and the command reports `0` with an explanation when the trading side is not deployed.

* [feature] Added the "События из трейдинга" block above the timeline of the lead card. It reads the five newest rows of the operator directly, so a manager sees a trade before the next minute of the feed, and it is inserted through `view_render_event('admin.leads.view.activities.before')` — the Admin package is not edited. Both entry points check `TradingEventRepository::available()` and stay silent on an installation without the shared table.

* [fix] The feed is idempotent by design: `TradingEventRepository::markProcessed()` reserves the event id with `insertOrIgnore` on the ledger primary key, and the reservation, the activity and the lead pivot are written in one transaction, so an overlapping run, a retry after a failure and a manual `artisan` call all leave exactly one timeline entry per event. An unknown event type is parked (a ledger row without an activity) instead of being retried on every run, while an event of an account without a lead is left unreserved and picked up by the next run.

* [feature] The feed is scheduled every minute with `withoutOverlapping()` from the provider (nothing is added to the core `routes/console.php`), and `php artisan schedule:list` shows it.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/TradingEventFeedTest.php`: the deposit, short and loss wording and their comments, the newest lead of an account, the ledger reservation on a repeated delivery, a parked unknown type, an account without a lead staying in the queue, the feed reaching the listener through the provider mapping, the contract binding, the lead-card block rendering the newest trade, and the amount formatting. The tests build the event model with `newFromBuilder()` instead of writing into the shared `trading_events` table, so they do not depend on the rows a development database carries or on the trading migrations being run there; the block test reads the real table and skips itself when the trading side is not deployed.

* [feature] The field names, the lookup options and the already saved activity history are now translated in the database, not in a lang file: `Config/localization.php` declares the `en` / `ru` pair of every shipped field (`leads`, `persons`, `organizations`, `products`, `quotes`, `warehouses`) and of every option of `lead_sources`, `lead_types`, `lead_pipelines` and `lead_pipeline_stages`, and `php artisan crypto-call-center:localize-catalog` writes it. Krayin resolves a column title from `attributes.name` and an option label from the lookup row, so until now the whole lead form and the activity history printed English on the Russian screens no matter what the translations said.

* [feature] The command is safe to re-run: a field is renamed only while it still carries its shipped English name, an option is matched by its English name, and a history entry that already names the Russian field is skipped, so a second run reports `0` on every line. A field an administrator renamed by hand keeps its own wording — the run only reports the drift — and `--force` replaces it anyway. `--dry-run` prints the plan without writing, `--skip-history` leaves the labels already frozen in the history alone.

* [fix] A history entry is resolved through the entity that owns it (`lead_activities`, `person_activities`, `product_activities`, `warehouse_activities`), so the shared "Name" of a product never gets the label of a person, and an entry whose entity is gone is rewritten only when the catalog declares the stored name exactly once. Only `type = 'system'` activities are touched, the payload keeps every other key, and `activities.title` is rewritten by a trailing match on the field name so "Updated: Source" becomes "Обновлено: Источник" while the surrounding wording the core wrote is preserved.
* [fix] A date field also froze its formatted label: `AttributeValueRepository::getAttributeLabel()` formats a date with `D M d, Y` (a datetime with `H:i A`) before it is stored, so the history kept printing "Tue Oct 20, 2026" after the field itself was translated. The raw value is in the payload, so the command rebuilds the label from it as `20.10.2026` (`20.10.2026 14:30` for a datetime); a value the parser rejects keeps the label it has, and an entry whose label already matches the rebuilt one is not rewritten again.



* [fix] The counters are reset in `handle()`, because the container reuses the command instance when it runs more than once in a single process (`Artisan::call()` in a loop, a test that runs the command twice) and the second run would otherwise report the totals of the first.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/CatalogLocalizationTest.php`: the field names and the options written by the command, a history entry whose field name, payload label and title are rewritten together, idempotency on a second run, a hand-renamed field kept unless `--force` is given, `--dry-run` writing nothing, `--skip-history` leaving the history alone, a shared field name resolved through the entity of the activity, a date label rebuilt from the raw value, and a value the parser cannot turn into a date keeping its stored label.

* [security] The "Call Center Operator" role is pinned to the `individual` data scope. Krayin keeps `view_permission` on the user and lets the Settings → Users form pick `global` / `group` / `individual`, so a role-level expectation has to be enforced where the user is written: `Config/roles.php` declares the scope, and `Support/OperatorScope` rewrites it from a `saving` listener registered by the provider, covering the admin form, importers, seeders and tinker alike. The lead list keeps filtering through the core `bouncer()->getAuthorizedUserIds()`, so an operator only ever sees the leads they own.

* [feature] `php artisan crypto-call-center:operator-role --sync-users` retrofits the packaged scope onto accounts created before it was declared; without the flag the command only reports how many accounts drift. A scope Krayin does not understand (anything outside `global` / `group` / `individual`) makes the command fail instead of provisioning a role with dead config.

* [security] Lead deletion is denied at both layers: the role never receives `leads.delete` / `leads.mass_delete`, the core lead grid only adds its row "Delete" action under `bouncer()->hasPermission('leads.delete')`, the package concern hides the mass action, and `DELETE admin/leads/{id}` answers 401 for an own and a foreign lead alike. No Blade override was needed.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/OperatorScopeTest.php`: the declared scope, the rewrite on create and update, other roles left untouched, the `--sync-users` retrofit, refusal of an unknown scope, a lead grid that lists only the operator's leads, the hidden row "Delete" action, and the refused `DELETE` requests.

* [feature] Declared the "Call Center Operator" role as package data (`Config/roles.php`): leads in view/edit, contacts in view, activities create/view and the wallets screen. `php artisan crypto-call-center:operator-role` provisions it idempotently - it creates the role once, leaves an existing one untouched unless `--sync` is passed, and accepts `--name` / `--description`. No ACL key was invented: the role is a subset of the tree the Admin package and this package already declare, and a key that disappears from `config('acl')` makes the command fail instead of leaving a dead permission behind.

* [fix] The role sees no forbidden section and no dead end: `Menu::getItems('admin')` already prints a node only for a granted key, so Settings, Users, Roles, Products, Quotes, Mail, Call history and the dashboard stay hidden. A role without `dashboard` is sent by `SessionController` to its first menu item after signing in, the branded header sends the logo there instead of to the dashboard, the `admin::partials.breadcrumbs` override drops the dashboard root the shipped trail is built on, and the `admin::errors.index` override points the error page home button at the first reachable section instead of looping back into a 401. A role that holds `dashboard` keeps the shipped behaviour on all four surfaces.

* [fix] Mass actions are no longer printed for a role that cannot run them. Krayin adds every grid mass action unconditionally, so the "Delete" button of the leads, activities and organizations screens was only refused by the middleware after the request had been sent. `DataGrids/Concerns/HidesUnauthorizedMassActions` resolves an action URL back to its route, looks the route up in `acl()->getRoles()` and asks `bouncer()->hasPermission()`; the package datagrids (`LeadDataGrid`, `ActivityDataGrid`, `OrganizationDataGrid`) are bound over the core ones, so every screen inherits the filter without a core edit.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/OperatorRoleTest.php`: provisioning from the config, idempotency and `--sync`, refusal of an unknown ACL key, menu composition, the reachable and refused screens (including `DELETE` on a lead), the breadcrumb and error page home links, and the mass actions printed for the operator versus an administrator.

* [feature] Added `Database/Seeders/DemoDataSeeder` and `php artisan crypto-call-center:demo-data`, so a fresh installation is not a set of empty screens: the run writes 10 contacts, 15 leads spread over all six pipeline stages (4 new, 3 follow up, 2 prospect, 2 negotiation, 2 won, 2 lost), 5 crypto wallets and 8 call logs. The rows go through the core repositories, so the `unique_id` of a person, its contact details and the activity entries Krayin writes on save are exactly the ones a manual save produces, and the kanban, the dashboard widgets, the wallets screen and the call history all have something to show.

* [feature] The demo rows are marked by their e-mail domain (`demo.klimiata.test`) instead of a bookkeeping table: a second run reports "Demo data is already present and was left untouched. Pass --fresh to replace it." and writes nothing, `--fresh` removes the rows of an earlier run first (leads, then their persons; the wallets and the calls follow the cascading foreign keys) and `--no-calls` seeds everything but the call history. The owner is the first account with a full-access role, the pipeline is the default one, and a missing administrator, pipeline, stage, source or type fails the run with an explanation rather than writing half of the set — every insert happens in one transaction. The counters are reset in `run()`, because the container reuses the seeder instance when it is run more than once in a single process.

* [feature] Added `tests/Feature/CryptoCallCenter/DemoDataSeederTest.php`: the seeded persons, leads, wallets and calls, the `lead_value` range and the open/closed split, the activity entries of a lead and of a person, the skip of a second run and the replacement on `--fresh`, the lead kanban and the dashboard rendering with the demo set, and the command with `--no-calls`. The helper `completeDemoPipeline()` adds the six stages to the pipeline `securityTestPipeline()` returns, because the parallel test databases are migrated but not seeded; the counts are scoped to the demo domain, because a development database may already hold the rows.
